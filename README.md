# property-investment-calculator
Browser-based Australian property investment calculator to work out whether buying an investment property is a financial masterstroke or an expensive way to become someone's landlord.

Enter your purchase price, deposit, mortgage details, rental income and a few assumptions about the future. It'll project your property value, mortgage balance, cash flow and potential capital gains tax over the life of the loan.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Free to use, modify and share. Just download the files and open index.html in your browser.

Disclaimer: This is not financial advice. The calculations are simplified, the assumptions are guesses about the future, and Australian tax law is considerably more complicated than a few lines of JavaScript. Don't make major financial decisions based on some bloke on GitHub.
