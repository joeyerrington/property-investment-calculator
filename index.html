Purchase year is the starting position, followed by one row for each mortgage year. Rent is received at the end of each year. Repayments use annual compounding and annual payments to match the original CSV. Net investment gain assumes sale at that year's value and includes cash flow to date, less the original deposit and buying costs. Estimated CGT is illustrative, not an annual tax payment. It assumes individual ownership, an eligible 50% discount after at least 12 months, and a fixed marginal rate. Tax deductions, depreciation, rental income tax, loan fees, changing expenses, land tax variations, capital works and detailed CGT eligibility rules are not modelled.
